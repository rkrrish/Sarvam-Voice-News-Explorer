    🌐 About this
    
          
    This is a voice-first, multilingual news research agent. Ask a question about any current event — by
            speaking or typing — in any Indian language, and the system fetches real news articles,
            composes a grounded answer with citations, and speaks it back to you in your own language.
          
    
        
    

        
        
    
          
    ⚙️ How it works (7 stages)
    
          
      
            
    1. 🎙️ Transcription — Your voice is converted to text (Sarvam saaras:v3)
      2. 
            
    2. 🌐 Translation — Non-English queries are translated to English, since NewsAPI only
              supports English searches (sarvam-translate:v1)
      3. 
            
    3.  🧠 Intent Analysis — The LLM understands what you want and generates an
              optimised search query (sarvam-105b-conversations)
      4. 
            
    4. 📡 News Retrieval — Up to 8 live articles are fetched from NewsAPI.org
      5. 
            
    5. ✍️ Grounded Answer — The LLM reads the articles and drafts a cited answer — it cannot
              fabricate facts (sarvam-105b-conversations)
      6. 
            
    6. 🔁 Back-Translation — The English answer is translated back into your language
              (sarvam-translate:v1)
      7. 
            
    7. 🔊 Voice Synthesis — A language-matched news anchor voice reads the answer aloud
              (bulbul:v3)
      8. 
          
    
        
    

        
        
    
          
    📌
    
          
    
            Every answer is grounded in real articles. You'll always see the exact news sources the AI
            used. Look for the "⭐ Used in Answer" badge on article cards, and click "Read full story
              ↗" on any card to open the original article and verify the facts yourself.
